Getting Started

Getting Started

Logging In

All of your website's content lives in the Studio. To get in:

  1. Go to your studio URL: irish-hospital-apartments.sanity.studio/structure
  2. Log in with the account you were invited with (Google, GitHub, or email/password).
  3. You'll land in the Manage Content tab — that's where you'll do everything below.

How Manage Content Works

All editing happens in the Manage Content tab. Open a list, pick the item you want to edit (or add a new one), fill in the fields, and publish. That's the whole workflow, whether you're updating the homepage or your site's search settings.

Drafts vs. Published

Every change you make is saved as a draft automatically. Drafts are not visible on the live website — only you can see them in the studio.

To make your changes live, click the Publish button at the bottom right of the editor. Changes appear on irishhospitalapartments.com within about a minute.

💡 Tip: Don't worry about breaking anything. Your drafts don't affect the live site until you hit Publish. Edit freely, then publish when it looks right.

Working with Images

To add or change an image:

  1. Click the image field.
  2. Click "Upload" to select a file from your computer, or drag and drop.
  3. Set a hotspot by clicking on the image and dragging the circle to the most important part. This ensures the image crops well on all screen sizes.
  • Use JPG or PNG format.
  • Keep file sizes under 2MB for best performance.
  • The system automatically optimizes images for fast loading.

Publishing

  1. Make your edits.
  2. Click "Publish" at the bottom right.
  3. Wait about a minute, then check the live site. If changes don't appear, do a hard refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R on PC or Cmd+Shift+R on Mac.
💡 Tip: Bookmark your studio URL so you can get back quickly: irish-hospital-apartments.sanity.studio/structure

Title Template Controls how page titles are formatted in the browser tab and search results. This is set up correctly — leave it as is.
Search Appearance
Default Title The main headline Google shows for your homepage.
50–60 characters
Default Description The short summary that appears under your title in Google search results.
150–160 characters
Keywords Words and phrases that describe your business — e.g. "apartments Forest City," "historic apartments Iowa."
5–10 keywords
Default OG Image The image shown when someone shares your website link on Facebook, Instagram, or in a text message.
1200 × 630px
Twitter / X Handle Your handle on X, including the @. Leave blank if you're not on there.
Business Details

Phone Number Your business phone number.
Email Address Your business email address.
Address Street, City, State, and ZIP — currently 216 S. Clark St., Forest City, IA 50436. Keep this current; it's what shows up in Google Maps and local search results.
Service Areas Cities or areas you serve — e.g. "Forest City," "Winnebago County." Helps you show up in nearby searches.
Price Range A general price indicator ($ to $$$$) shown in some search results.
